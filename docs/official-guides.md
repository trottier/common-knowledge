# Official Ready.gov guides

The publisher permits organizations to reproduce Ready Campaign publications
unchanged, including their pictures and graphics, provided the distribution does
not imply FEMA or U.S. Government endorsement. The exact proof page is
https://www.ready.gov/publications; the catalog records that page for each file.
`LicenseRef-FEMA-Ready-Reprint` names these terms locally. It does not claim that
all embedded images are public domain or that this is an open-content license.

Each file is fetched directly from the PDF link on an official collection page.
The source bytes, original title and agency credit remain intact. These files
are placed under `4_PHONE_PACK/official/<edition>/`, with a no-endorsement notice
in the catalog attribution. They are not regenerated, translated or placed in an
in-depth knowledge collection. The catalog rejects applying this grant to
regenerated items, other hosts or collection placements.

The development capture on 2026-11-01 contains **40 PDFs**, **268 pages** and
**103,313,785 bytes**. The publisher did not supply SHA-256 sidecars; the downloaded
bytes were hashed, parsed as unencrypted PDFs and checked for root open actions.
All pins and source-currency checks remain marked for independent human review.
This check does not establish clinical accuracy, complete PDF active-content
analysis or readability on physical phones. Official Letter-style documents are
supplementary; the project's phone PDFs remain the core.

The six source collections are [Emergency Supply List](https://www.ready.gov/collection/emergency-supply-list),
[Are You Ready?](https://www.ready.gov/collection/are-you-ready),
[12 Ways to Prepare](https://www.ready.gov/collection/12-ways-to-prepare),
[Earthquakes](https://www.ready.gov/collection/earthquakes),
[Power Outage](https://www.ready.gov/collection/power-outage) and
[Wildfire](https://www.ready.gov/collection/wildfire).
The English supply list here is the file linked by that collection; other Ready
pages link different revisions, which have not been silently substituted.

The site warns that these publications reflect information up to September 30,
2025 and will not receive regular updates, even though page modification dates
can be later. Independent currency review therefore remains required. The
absence of Farsi, Polish, Portuguese or Hindi files in these six collections does
not establish that the agencies have no materials in those languages. Research
of other official collections remains open. The requested 15-edition selection
has not changed, and the Japanese list was not added.
