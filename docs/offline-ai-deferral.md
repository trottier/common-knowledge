# Offline AI is deferred

The publisher's current milestone is one small-state Variant 1 sample without
offline AI. `config/variants.yaml` records both the future allocation
`offline_ai: [7, 8]` and the explicit current state
`offline_ai_enabled: false`. No current variant selects an AI model, Android AI
app, or generated AI bundle. Neither AI folder is created during deferral.

The five previously placed entries are now held `hub_only` records:
`base_model`, `sample_model`, `local_llm_full`, `local_llm_sample`, and
`offlinellm_android_candidate`. Their original URLs, hashes, source graphs,
legal/review fields, and historical destinations remain intact. The verified
97 outputs and receipts remain historical research; their pins are not
relabelled for changed source code or newly integrated into this milestone.

The planner also rejects accidental AI placement while disabled. If integration
is enabled in a future change, placement remains restricted to the configured
future allocation. Capacity overflow fails normally; there is no universal or
four-variant fallback. Enabling the flag alone does not activate held catalog
items or approve their rights, sources, or devices.

English and Spanish START, card, and variant-explanation text now give a short
future-only note whose drive numbers come from configuration. Spanish retains
machine status and updated English-body hashes. No reviewer, review date, or
review requirement is cleared. Current START contains no model-loading or AI
launcher instructions.

This change deliberately preserves the older research builders, launcher names,
and stored outputs. Future integration must implement the final `9_OFFLINE_AI`
layout, terminology, warnings, complete source/notices, device checks, and fresh
measured builds. No AI build, signing, device check, or release approval occurs
as part of deferral. The generic executable policy recognizes the future folder
and rejects the old direct `9_LOCAL_LLM` executable location.

Configuration and core-source fingerprints change. Build the sample core from
the final combined source before assembly. Existing verified non-AI artifacts
can retain their actual historical recipes and hashes; this change does not
rename or relabel their provenance.
