# CDC children's preparedness and coping books

Fifteen original Ready Wrigley / Listo Calixto PDFs were captured directly from
CDC Stacks on 2026-11-01: 300 pages and 35,022,722 source bytes. Each individual
record explicitly states **Rights: Public Domain**. Every downloaded PDF matched
its publisher's SHA-512 checksum; the catalog also pins the measured SHA-256,
size, original title, publication date, rights proof and checksum-source page.
Publisher checksums and per-item rights were checked separately; no general
assumption about all CDC-hosted files was used.

The English and Spanish coping books are placed through the care and
psychosocial topics. If both topics occur on one variant, the existing assembler
places one copy in the first matching topic alphabetically. The other thirteen
books form a small preparedness/activity supplement to the school collection;
they are not a complete school curriculum. Topics cover earthquakes, extreme
heat, hurricanes, storm/flood recovery, tornadoes, wildfires/smoke and winter.
The storm/flood recovery record did not link a Spanish counterpart; none was
invented or inferred from a filename.

The PDF bytes and all original author, illustrator and agency credits remain
unchanged. In particular, the coping books retain the American Academy of
Pediatrics collaboration credit. Distribution does not imply agency endorsement.
These are original publisher page layouts, which may require zooming on a phone;
the project's phone PDFs remain the core. All files parsed as unencrypted PDFs
without root open actions. That check is not a complete active-content analysis
or a physical device test.

Publication dates span older source editions. Independent source-currency,
child-suitability, per-item rights/pin and physical readability reviews remain
explicit catalog gates. Their archival availability does not establish current
clinical or emergency guidance. No human review status is granted here.
