# Materials excluded pending a written license

The revised specification excludes Red Cross, Stop the Bleed, Hesperian,
Khan Academy and iFixit until each grants a free emergency-use license in writing.
None is selected for the drive. No permission request has been sent by the agent.
The iFixit candidate has been removed from the active catalog and capacity plan.

Current PhET simulations are also excluded. The publisher's
[current licensing page](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/licensing), checked on
2026-10-31, uses CC BY-NC 4.0 and explicitly treats government and institutional
distribution programs as commercial uses requiring a separate license. Older
translated FAQs do not establish the license of a current download. No PhET
archive is selected. This does not determine the rights of an individually
identified older release; any such proposal needs its own dated license proof.

A legal review of a noncommercial license is insufficient. A future proposal must
include the rights holder's written grant, its scope, proof URL and a named human
review before the material enters the active catalog. The collection must be
redistributable on the free government-distributed drive without further permission.

Army publications remain a separate review: confirm federal-work status and
distribution statement A for each included work. The local LLM's exact file and
redistribution license also require confirmation before selection.
