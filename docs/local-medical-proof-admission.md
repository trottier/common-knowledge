# Local medical-framing proof admission

`python -m tools.admit_medical_proof` creates a fresh local inspection clone.
It authenticates the source tree/ancestor, the historical generated medical pack
against its unchanged catalog pin, the new framing generation and full audit,
producer source hashes, and every original/new clinical HTML/PDF association.
Every original `<main>` must remain byte-identical. Exact article membership,
source metadata, font pins, epochs, and required notices remain bound.

Only those generated medical paths change; source/license notices stay unchanged.
The two named project draft notes may move byte-identically into laptop Source
notes when present. A stale copy index is removed, not relabeled. No new index
is generated until the final folder organization is ready. START/phone-pack
backups, the PDF byte budget, and the self-excluding manifest are checked.

The external provenance retains original and new generations separately. The
new framing remains a proof with existing review holds; it does not replace the
historical catalog pin, pass normal sample assembly, or grant release approval.
The copy-index tools also list actual ZIM/OBF files. Companion PBF source and
required notices must remain with map folders even though they are not index rows.
