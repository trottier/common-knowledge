## Open the drive

Plug the drive into your phone or laptop.
Open the drive in the phone's Files app or your laptop's file manager.
Open `1_START`, then choose your language. [@project_spec]

Start with the phone PDF on a phone.
Choose the print PDF when printing on US Letter paper. [@project_spec]
