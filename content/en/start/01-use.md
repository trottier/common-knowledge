## iPhone

Connect the drive. Use an adapter that fits your phone if needed.
Open Files. Tap Browse, then choose the drive under Locations.
Open `1_START`, then your language folder. [@apple_usb]

Start with the phone PDF on a phone.
Choose the print PDF when printing on US Letter paper. [@project_spec]

Do not force a connector that does not fit.
If the drive is missing, check the connection and available power. [@apple_usb]

## Android

Connect the drive. Open your built-in Files app and look for USB storage.
In Files by Google, accept the USB prompt and open the storage device.
Open `1_START`, then your language folder. [@google_usb]

[[VERIFY: test the built-in Samsung, Motorola and Pixel file managers; confirm no app installation is needed.]]

## Laptop

Open the drive in Finder or your file manager.
Start with the PDF in `1_START`. Optional programs are in `7_LAPTOP`. [@project_spec]

## Keep a copy

Copy `4_PHONE_PACK` to your phone's local storage when there is room.
Do not move or delete the drive's original files. [@project_spec]

On iPhone, open a copied PDF in Files. Use Share, then AirDrop, to send it to a nearby Apple device. [@apple_airdrop]
On Android, select a copied file and choose Share, then Quick Share. [@google_share]

Do not send private medical information to a shared public device.
