## Get to a safer place

Check for fire, falling objects, damaged wires and gas smells.
Do not enter a damaged building to retrieve this drive.
[[VERIFY: safety reviewer must confirm this scene-safety checklist against current federal guidance.]]

## Check people

Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency if you can.
Text 911 if you cannot call; text service is not available everywhere. [@calling_911]
For severe bleeding or a person who is not breathing normally, open **Medical basics** now. [@bleeding] [@cpr]

Do not assume the places directory lists open services.
It lists places that may help, not confirmed openings. [@project_spec]

Do not cross fire, floodwater or other hazards to reach a meeting.

## If 911 does not answer

Keep giving the first aid described under **Medical basics** when it is safe.
Ask a neighbor to keep trying for help while someone stays with the injured person.
