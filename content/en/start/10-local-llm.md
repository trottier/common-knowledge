## Experimental local LLM

Experimental local LLM. It can be wrong. If it disagrees with a card or video, follow the card. [@project_spec]

The model and launchers are in `9_LOCAL_LLM` when included by the plan.
Use the launcher for your laptop's operating system.
On Android, follow the guide beside the optional app. [@project_spec]

Do not use a local LLM answer to guess a dose, replace a clinician's advice or decide that a building is safe.
Ask it to point to a START section, then read that section yourself. [@project_spec]
