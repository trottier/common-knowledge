## Gas

[[VERIFY: utility safety reviewer must approve a national gas shutoff procedure, including when not to shut off, safe access, valve types and when only the gas company may restore service.]]

The gas tag is a label, not an instruction to shut off every meter.
Do not practice by turning the gas off. [@fema_utilities]

## Generator carbon monoxide

Put a portable generator outdoors, at least 20 feet from doors, windows and vents.
Never run it in a home or garage, even with doors open. [@cdc_generator]

[[VERIFY: add approved instructions for exhaust direction, refueling, dry placement, CO alarms and preventing backfeed.]]

## If emergency help does not answer

Stay out of the hazard area.
