# Third-party materials

No external drive cargo is cataloged or distributed at the scaffold stage.
Catalog validation and generation of this report arrive in work-order step 5.
Imported materials will retain their own licenses and attribution; the project's
Apache-2.0 and CC0-1.0 licenses do not relicense them.

Build and test dependencies are recorded in `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock` and
`Dockerfile.build`. The lock includes publisher artifact URLs and hashes. These
tools are not cargo placed on the emergency drive.
